About CartWise

Smart Shopping Made Simple

CartWise is a simple e-commerce platform designed to make online shopping easy, organized and convenient.

Users can explore different product categories, search for products, view product details, add items to their cart or wishlist, and complete the checkout process through a simple interface.

CartWise also includes a chatbot assistant that helps users with basic product and shopping-related queries.

Why Choose CartWise?

Wishlist & Cart

Shopping Assistant

The integrated chatbot provides quick answers to basic product and shopping queries.
